Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the MediBox system

What is this Medical Box system?

MediBox IoT is an intelligent medication adherence assistant. It connects a physical hardware dispenser box (fitted with 3 scheduled indicator lights, a loud buzzer alarm, and a patient confirmation button) to this live web platform. It ensures patients take their pills at the exact prescribed hours while automatically logging response times and adherence statistics.

How do I use this system? (Step-by-Step)

1

Connect the Hardware: Plug your ESP32 box into your PC with a USB data cable and click "Connect USB" in the top header.

2

Set Dose Times: Click "Schedule" or "Edit Times" to configure your Morning, Afternoon, and Night reminder hours in simple 12-Hour AM/PM format.

3

Alarm Reminder: When dose time arrives, the matching light flashes and the buzzer sounds for 10 seconds.

4

Take Pill & Confirm: Take your medication and press the physical button on the box (or click "Confirm Intake" on this screen) within 10 seconds.

5

Track Adherence: Review your adherence rate, historical logs, and timing trends anytime right here.

What do the 3 indicator lights (LEDs) mean?

🟢 Morning (GPIO 19)

Flashes for morning dose (default 08:00 AM).

🟡 Afternoon (GPIO 18)

Flashes for afternoon dose (default 01:30 PM).

🔴 Night (GPIO 5)

Flashes for evening/night dose (default 08:00 PM).

What happens during an alarm? (10-Second Rule)

When a reminder time triggers, the buzzer sounds and the active LED flashes. You have 10 seconds to press the button:

  • If pressed on time: The buzzer immediately silences, the OLED screen smiles with "TAKEN ON TIME! :)", and the dashboard marks the dose as TAKEN.
  • If 10 seconds elapse with no press: The buzzer stops, the screen displays "MISSED! :(", and the dashboard records a MISSED alert so you or a caregiver can follow up.

How does the direct USB Cable link work?

This dashboard uses the modern Web Serial API supported natively in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Opera. When you click "Connect USB", your browser opens a high-speed direct COM port link (115200 baud) to the ESP32. This enables instant bidirectional hardware control even when this website is hosted online on Vercel or GitHub!

What does the Adherence Rate mean?

The adherence percentage in the circular donut gauge measures your consistency:
• 80% – 100%: Optimal / Healthy (You consistently take medicines on time).
• 50% – 79%: Moderate (Some doses missed, room for improvement).
• Below 50%: Low Adherence (Frequent missed medication, needs attention).

Can I test the lights and buzzer right now?

Yes! In the DOSE SCHEDULE panel, click the small Play (▶) icon on Morning, Afternoon, or Night. It will immediately trigger a 10-second test alarm on your physical box (and this website) so you can test without waiting for the scheduled hour!

Can I correct a mistaken status?

Yes. In the Activity Log table at the bottom, every recorded dose has a switch button. If you pressed the button by accident or took the pill 1 minute later, click the switch button to toggle between TAKEN and MISSED.
